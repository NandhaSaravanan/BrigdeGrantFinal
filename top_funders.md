## Caveats

- **Invite-only labels:** For most small family foundations, INVITE-ONLY is a judgment from their giving history, not a stated rule. The 990-PF "preselected organizations only" box wasn't checked one by one. In this data, a funder with one or two gifts to a church or a family-connected charity is unlikely to fund new applicants.
- **Inactive:** Two funders are INACTIVE: Grace and Hope Foundation (last 990 FY2020) and Kristie Lee Tautenhahn Foundation (last 990 FY2021).
- **Not actually grantmakers:** Three rows don't look like real grantmakers. EIN 541594578 (row 107) now belongs to Christian Relief Services Charities, EIN 561514058 (row 118) shows as Early Years Inc in NC, and Old Town Association doesn't make grants.
- **No guessed deadlines:** "Not published" means no official date was found, and nothing was guessed.
